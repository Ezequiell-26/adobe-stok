# SKILL — PORTFOLIO DIVERSIFICATION AND SIMILARITY CONTROL

## Goal

Build a portfolio rather than a pile of nearly identical AI outputs.

## Similarity test

Two assets are not materially distinct merely because they have:

- a different crop;
- a different aspect ratio;
- a flipped/rotated composition;
- a small color change;
- a filter;
- a minor retouch;
- a small zoom;
- a changed background hue.

Meaningful distinction should affect the concept, story, context, buyer use case, subject relationship, composition or environment.

## Portfolio dimensions

Diversify across:

- subject matter;
- industries;
- life situations;
- demographics where appropriate;
- geography/cultural context;
- camera viewpoint;
- composition;
- vertical/horizontal utility;
- emotional/narrative intent;
- design use case.

## Batch policy

When producing multiple assets from one research theme, create a concept map first. Select only the strongest and sufficiently differentiated concepts.

The current Adobe limit/guidance on similar generative submissions must be checked on the official page before a batch. Do not hard-code an outdated fixed number.

## Internal ledger

For each asset store:

`ASSET_ID | CONCEPT | USE_CASE | SUBJECT | COMPOSITION | STATUS`

Before READY, compare against the ledger and the intended submission set.
