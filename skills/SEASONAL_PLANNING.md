# SEASONAL PLANNING — Adobe Stock

## Purpose
Predictably prepare concepts before commercial demand peaks without treating predictions as facts.

## Timing model
For each concept classify:
- `EVERGREEN` — recurring need with no narrow date dependency;
- `SEASONAL` — demand linked to a recurring period;
- `EVENT_DRIVEN` — tied to a known upcoming event, launch, cultural moment, or brief;
- `EMERGING` — recent signal with uncertain persistence.

For seasonal/event-driven concepts:
1. identify likely buyer lead time;
2. research current signals;
3. create before peak demand when practical;
4. avoid depicting real news as authentic when the asset is generative;
5. re-check the concept immediately before production.

## Staleness
A calendar assumption is never enough. Revalidate the actual market signal before production.

## Portfolio balance
Prefer a controlled mix of evergreen, seasonal, emerging, and evidence-backed opportunity concepts rather than concentrating the entire portfolio in one trend.
