# SKILL — PROFESSIONAL STOCK PHOTOGRAPHY

## Goal

Make generated imagery behave like a deliberate professional commercial photograph, not an AI illustration pretending to be photography.

## Art direction

Every image needs one clear visual idea. Remove details that do not improve the buyer's use case.

Define before generation:

- subject;
- action/state;
- environment;
- time/season;
- camera viewpoint;
- focal length/look;
- focus plane;
- depth of field;
- key light;
- fill/ambient light;
- shadow direction;
- color treatment;
- material behavior;
- negative space;
- intended copy area;
- buyer crop/use cases.

## Lens / camera logic

Use photographic language that corresponds to the scene. Do not stuff prompts with contradictory camera terms.

Examples of coherent directions:

- lifestyle: natural eye-level perspective, moderate focal length, realistic depth;
- product: controlled perspective, clean separation, physically plausible studio lighting;
- environmental portrait: moderate wide/normal view, natural background compression;
- food: close framing, controlled depth of field, realistic highlights and texture;
- architecture/interior: corrected perspective, believable verticals and spatial scale.

## Lighting

Light must have an understandable source and consistent direction.

Check:

- key-to-fill relationship;
- shadow softness;
- contact shadows;
- rim light consistency;
- reflected light;
- exposure roll-off;
- color temperature;
- highlight behavior.

Avoid generic “dramatic cinematic lighting” unless the actual scene supports it.

## Materials

Every visible material needs believable response to light.

Glass: reflection/refraction should follow geometry.

Metal: highlights should move with curvature.

Fabric: weave, folds and seams should respond to tension.

Skin: pores/subsurface texture should be natural, not waxy.

Food: texture must match the food type and moisture.

Water/liquids: gravity, meniscus, reflections and transparency should be coherent.

Ice: translucent geometry, cracks and melt behavior should be plausible.

## Composition

Prefer:

- simple visual hierarchy;
- strong focal point;
- believable scale;
- clean edges;
- copy space where valuable;
- crop tolerance;
- no accidental tangencies;
- no clutter that reduces licensing usefulness.

## Premium stock standard

The target is:

**specific concept + authentic photographic behavior + controlled composition + clean post-production + broad commercial usability.**

Avoid the visual fingerprints of low-quality AI stock:

- over-perfect plastic skin;
- glossy everything;
- excessive shallow depth of field;
- fake bokeh;
- impossible reflections;
- overly saturated colors;
- excessive sharpness;
- generic luxury aesthetic pasted onto unrelated concepts;
- decorative objects with no narrative purpose.

## People

Direct subjects naturally. Avoid identical expressions, cloned faces, rigid fingers, perfect symmetry and stiff poses.

Hands must be checked separately because small defects can survive thumbnail inspection.

## Generic product photography

Use unbranded fictional products when possible. Product geometry must be internally consistent. Labels should be blank or generic unless intentionally created and verified.

## Editorial-layout-like utility

For commercial layout utility, consider intentional negative space on one side and stable background texture. Do not call generated imagery authentic editorial/news documentation.

## Prompt construction

Use this order:

`commercial purpose → subject → action → environment → composition → camera/lens → lighting → material behavior → color → negative space → realism → constraints`

Avoid endless adjective chains. Specific photographic instructions are more useful than generic hype words.
