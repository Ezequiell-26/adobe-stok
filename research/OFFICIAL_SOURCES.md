## Verification protocol

For a fresh session:

1. Open the relevant current official Adobe pages.
2. Record `URL`, `page title`, `last updated date` if shown, and the specific rule used.
3. Prefer the newest page over repository text when they conflict.
4. If a page cannot be verified, do not present a current policy claim as fact.
5. Trend collections are evidence of Adobe's current editorial curation, not audited sales rankings.
