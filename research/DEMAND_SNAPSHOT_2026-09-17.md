## How to use these signals

Do NOT turn a trend name into a generic stock cliché.

Use the trend as a starting signal, then derive a specific buyer problem, audience, message, setting and composition. Prefer a concept that is difficult to find, commercially useful, visually obvious, and technically achievable without IP risk.
