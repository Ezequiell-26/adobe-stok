# Adobe Stock Rules — Operational Consolidation

Reference date: 2026-09-17.

Adobe Stock permits generative-AI content from contributors under its current
rules, subject to correct AI labeling, rights, technical requirements,
content/IP policies, metadata, and moderation.

## Mandatory operational rules

- Declare generative AI in the Adobe contributor portal when applicable.
- Verify the AI tool's license/terms permit commercial stock use.
- Use releases when actually required.
- Avoid real people/celebrity likenesses unless rights are properly covered.
- Avoid logos, trademarks, brands, recognizable branded packaging, protected
  characters, protected creative works, and copied campaigns/designs.
- Avoid names of artists/real people/characters/brands as prompt or metadata
  bait.
- Do not submit generative work as if it were authentic news documentation.
- Do not use misleading metadata.
- Avoid large sets of near-identical iterations; choose materially distinct
  and commercially useful assets.
- Review the final asset at 100%.
- For photos, prepare a compliant final JPEG in sRGB, 4–100 MP, <=45 MB,
  sharp, correctly exposed, without watermarks/timestamps/branding.

## Important

No workflow can guarantee acceptance. Adobe has final moderation authority and
policies can change. Recheck the official source pages before high-volume
submission.
