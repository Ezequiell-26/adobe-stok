# Rejection Gates

An asset must pass ALL gates.

## G0 — Concept
- commercial usefulness
- original concept
- no third-party IP dependency

## G1 — Prompt safety
- no brands
- no artists
- no real-person names
- no characters
- no protected work references
- no copied campaign/product instructions

## G2 — Visual integrity
- no material AI artifacts
- correct geometry
- correct anatomy when applicable
- physically coherent lighting/shadows/reflections
- correct material behavior

## G3 — Technical
- JPEG
- sRGB
- 4–100 MP
- <=45 MB
- sharp/exposed correctly

## G4 — Legal / license
- commercial use is permitted by the generation tool/license
- rights are documented
- releases are handled when necessary

## G5 — Metadata
- truthful title
- relevant keywords
- no IP keyword stuffing
- correct category

## G6 — Portal
- AI disclosure selected
- fictional people/property disclosure used when applicable
- releases attached when necessary

## G7 — Similarity
- not a near-duplicate of assets already submitted
- not part of an abusive repetitive series

Any FAIL => do not upload.
Any unresolved legal/license question => HOLD.
